🇺🇸 For USA Summer Internships

Don’t only prepare LeetCode.

For USA internship recruiting, I’d structure your preparation roughly like:

Area	Target
Easy LeetCode	40–50
Medium	150–180
Hard	20–30
SQL	20–30
OOP	Strong
DBMS	Strong
OS	Strong
Computer Networks	Strong
System Design	Basic
Projects	2–3 excellent
Resume	1-page
Behavioral	Prepared

The most important LeetCode distribution is approximately:

Easy → 20%
Medium → 70%
Hard → 10%

Don’t chase Hard problems just to increase your solved count.

The real goal

Don’t aim for:

“I solved 500 LeetCode problems.”

Aim for:

“Give me an unfamiliar Medium problem and I can identify the pattern, explain my approach, code it cleanly, analyze complexity, test edge cases, and communicate while solving.”

That’s much closer to what interviews actually test.

If you’re starting from scratch, I’d personally make your first milestone 150 problems, then do those same 150 again under timed conditions rather than immediately jumping to 300.
